--- INSTRUCTIONS ---

Produce a JSON object with ALL of these fields:

1. match_percentage (int 0-100): How well this ROLE suits the candidate at this career stage.
   Score the fit of the role to the candidate, not the density of tool names in the
   posting.

   Score these, in this order, and let each one pull the number down:

   a) Role scope. If the role is outside backend / ML / AI / GenAI — front-end,
      mobile, full-stack-first, embedded, product, solutions, sales, GTM, security,
      DevRel, technical writing, support, management — score it BELOW 50
      regardless of how many tools it names. A high tool count is not evidence the
      role is a fit; it is often the reason an out-of-scope role looks attractive.
      Scope is a gate, not a weight.

   b) Stage fit. The candidate is a final-year undergraduate with a two-month
      internship. A role that wants 3+ years of commercial experience is not a
      better match for having a longer requirements list — it is a worse match.
      Score roles that explicitly suit interns, new graduates, or students highly,
      even when the posting's stack is unfamiliar. Conversely, do not award a high
      score to a role whose seniority is well beyond the candidate.

   c) Evidence. Credit the candidate's ACTUAL documented work — named projects,
      measured outcomes, specific technologies they have shipped. Do not credit a
      posting for mentioning tools the candidate has never touched.

   d) Gaps. A role needing a substantial unlearnable stack the candidate has no
      evidence for should land in the 40s-50s, not the 70s. Reasonable stretch:
   50-70. Strong documented overlap: 70+.

2. letter_grade (str): A (≥90) / B (≥80) / C (≥70) / D (≥60) / F (<60)
